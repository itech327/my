- gases: stomata on the leaf; CO₂ chips stream in, O₂ chips stream out.

- iodine: iodine drops on bread (blue-black) and paper (yellow-brown).
- foil: half-covered leaf, 6 h of light, foil off, iodine: uncovered half blue-black, covered half yellow-brown; conclusion.
- q2: plant with CO₂? and H₂O?; question card (raw materials).
- why: three cards: O₂ for breathing, absorbs CO₂, start of food chains; protect green plants.
